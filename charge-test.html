Your cable rankings 🏁

📏 For a fair comparison
  • Test every cable in the same battery range (e.g. always 30–60%). Charging naturally slows above ~80%.
  • Keep the phone, screen brightness and starting % as similar as you can between tests.
  • Longer tests are more accurate — the battery sensor only reports whole %, so 2–5 minutes gives a steadier reading.
  • Let the phone cool between tests; a hot phone throttles charging.

Device & battery info

How this works & honest limits

This measures how quickly your battery percentage rises while charging, then estimates the current (mA) and power (watts) reaching the battery using the capacity you enter. It's the fair way to rank chargers & cables.

A web page cannot read the cable's data-transfer speed or the exact USB-PD wattage your charger negotiated — the browser has no access to that hardware detail. The watt figure here is a battery-side estimate (using ~3.85 V nominal), so treat it as a comparison number, not a lab measurement. On iPhone/Safari the battery sensor is unavailable, so use Manual mode.
